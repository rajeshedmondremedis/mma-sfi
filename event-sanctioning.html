National Framework

Legitimate. Structured. Safe.

MMA-SFI is the sole national governing body providing official sanctioning for Mixed Martial Arts competitions across India, ensuring athlete safety, certified officiating, medical readiness, and genuine ranking value.

Unsanctioned events put fighters at risk and lack official recognition. When an event is sanctioned by MMA-SFI, bout results count toward national selection, fighters receive comprehensive medical oversight, and matches adhere to unified international amateur rules.

Key Pillars of Sanctioned Tournaments

Medical Oversight

Mandatory pre-fight medical checks, on-site cage doctor, licensed cutmen, and emergency ambulance standby.

Certified Officials

AMMA & GAMMA certified referees, judges, timekeepers, and inspectors ensuring impartial decision-making.

Official Ranking

Athletic victories translate directly into official state and national rankings and Team India trial invitations.

Fair Weight Divisions

Strict weigh-in monitoring and equipment specifications (gloves, shin guards, cage padding) protecting combatants.

How to Apply for Event Sanctioning

  • Submission: Submit event date, venue layout, proposed fight card, and safety plan at least 45 days prior to event date.
  • Technical Review: MMA-SFI Technical Committee reviews arena dimensions, medical facilities, and fighter match-ups.
  • Official Assignment: Federation appoints certified Chief Referee, judges, supervisor, and doping control officers.
  • Sanction Grant: Official letter of sanction and federation branding provided for public promotion and broadcaster compliance.
