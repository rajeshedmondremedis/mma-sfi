Who We Are

Laying the Foundation for Indian MMA

MMA-SFI (Mixed Martial Arts Sports Federation of India) is the national governing body for MMA in India. We are committed to building a transparent, athlete-first structure that supports Indian fighters from the grassroots to the global stage.

Affiliated with AMMA (Asian Mixed Martial Arts Association) and GAMMA (Global Authority for Mixed Martial Arts), MMA-SFI operates with international standards to ensure fair competition, certified coaching and officiating, and fighter welfare at every level.

We aim to organise sanctioned district, state, and national-level championships, provide training and certification for officials, and work to create legitimate pathways for Indian athletes to represent the country in continental and world championships.

"At our core, we believe that Indian fighters deserve more than just a platform — they deserve a system that supports their dreams with structure, safety, and opportunity."

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a strong, structured future for Indian MMA — where fighters from every background get the opportunity, resources, and recognition they deserve.

We aim to uplift Indian athletes by supporting them with quality coaching, scientific training methods, proper nutrition, medical safety, and access to national and international platforms. Our goal is clear: to develop world-class fighters who bring medals home and make India a respected powerhouse in global MMA.

Our Mission

MMA-SFI is committed to developing a strong, transparent, and athlete-focused structure for Mixed Martial Arts in India.

We aim to uplift Indian fighters by setting national standards, certifying coaches and officials, organising fair competitions, and ensuring access to quality training, nutrition, and medical care. Our mission is to create a clear pathway for Indian athletes to grow from grassroots to global platforms — with integrity, safety, and excellence at every step.

Strategic Pillars

Core Objectives of MMA-SFI

The key institutional goals guiding the development, safety, and governance of mixed martial arts across all Indian states.

1

Talent Development

Identify and nurture MMA athletes from grassroots to elite level across all regions of India, establishing academies and junior training programs.

2

Standardised Coaching & Officiating

Build a national framework for certifying coaches, referees, cutmen, and judges to ensure fairness, safety, and consistency across all sanctioned bouts.

3

Structured Competitions

Organise and sanction MMA events at district, state, and national levels with transparent rankings, official weight brackets, and selection trials.

4

Fighter Welfare

Ensure fighters have access to proper training facilities, nutrition guidance, injury management, mandatory medical clearance, insurance, and mental health support.

5

International Exposure

Facilitate opportunities for Indian athletes to compete and succeed on continental and world championship platforms under recognized global federations.

6

Inclusion & Accessibility

Promote equal opportunities for athletes regardless of gender, background, or region, ensuring Mixed Martial Arts reaches every corner and talent in India.
