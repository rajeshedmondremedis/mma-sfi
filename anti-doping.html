Anti-Doping Policy

Home Integrity Anti-Doping Policy

We Fight Clean. Always.

At MMA-SFI, we believe the true spirit of mixed martial arts lies in discipline, skill, and fair competition — not artificial shortcuts. Doping undermines the integrity of the sport, endangers athlete health, and disrespects every fighter who trains honestly.

Our anti-doping policy adheres to the strict code of the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA), National Anti-Doping Agency (NADA), and our international affiliates GAMMA and AMMA. Every athlete competing in an MMA-SFI sanctioned event is subject to anti-doping protocols.

What Our Anti-Doping Policy Covers

  • Adherence to WADA Prohibited List: MMA-SFI enforces the latest international standards on prohibited substances and methods, updated annually.
  • Testing Protocols: Mandatory in-competition testing at all National Championships and Selection Trials, alongside unannounced out-of-competition testing pools.
  • Therapeutic Use Exemptions (TUE): Clear formal procedures for athletes who require documented medications that fall on the prohibited list for legitimate medical conditions.
  • Education & Awareness: Compulsory anti-doping awareness seminars for athletes, national coaches, cutmen, and medical staff before each competitive season.

Athlete Responsibilities & Strict Liability

Under international anti-doping regulations, the rule of Strict Liability applies to every athlete.

"I Didn't Know" is NOT a Valid Excuse

You as an athlete are strictly and solely responsible for any substance found in your biological specimen. Whether a contaminated supplement, tainted energy drink, or unverified prescription was taken intentionally or accidentally, a violation has occurred.

  • Be knowledgeable of and comply with all applicable anti-doping policies.
  • Take full responsibility in the context of anti-doping for what you ingest, inject, or use.
  • Inform medical personnel of your obligation not to use prohibited substances and ensure any medical treatment does not violate anti-doping rules.
  • Submit to sample collection immediately upon notification by authorized doping control officers.

Sanctions for Violations

Failure to comply with testing, tampering with samples, or testing positive for prohibited substances triggers immediate disciplinary action:

Suspension

Immediate provisional suspension from all MMA-SFI sanctioned events, state championships, and camps.

Disqualification

Forfeiture of bout results, tournament points, medals, prize earnings, and championship titles.

National Ban

Stripped of Team India representative eligibility, with multi-year or lifetime bans for repeat violations.

A Message to Athletes

"You may win with shortcuts. But you don’t deserve to.
Every punch you throw, every round you survive — let it be earned.
Because real fighters fight clean."
