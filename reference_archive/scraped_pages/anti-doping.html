Anti-Doping Policy – MMA SFI

Home – Anti-Doping Policy – MMA SFI

We Fight Clean. Always.

MMA is built on discipline, honour and fair play. At MMA-SFI, we believe that no victory is worth compromising your integrity. Our Anti-Doping Policy ensures that every athlete competing under our banner plays by the same rules without shortcuts, without banned substances and without
excuses.

We are committed to protecting clean athletes, creating a level playing field and promoting long-term athlete health and safety.

What Our Anti-Doping Policy Covers

  • Prohibited Substances & Methods : 
    MMA-SFI follows the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) Prohibited List. All athletes must avoid performance-enhancing drugs, masking agents or any method that manipulates testing or results.
  • Testing at Events :
    Doping control may be conducted at any MMA-SFI sanctioned event including National Championships, State Championships and official selection trials. Testing may be
    random or targeted.
  • Out-of-Competition Testing :
    Athletes selected for international representation or elite training programs may betested at any time, without prior notice.
  • Therapeutic Use Exemptions (TUE) :
    If you are required to take a medication that contains a banned substance for medical reasons, you must apply for a TUE in advance with proper documentation.
  • Education & Awareness : 
    MMA-SFI conducts regular sessions to educate athletes, coaches and support staff about anti-doping rules, safe supplement practices and the long-term risks of banned substances.

Athlete Responsibilities

If you compete under MMA-SFI, it is your responsibility to:

Submit to testing if selected and cooperate fully with officials

Disclose all medications in advance and apply for a TUE when needed

Refuse pressure or shortcuts offered by coaches, teammates or outsiders

Know what substances and methods are banned

Avoid all supplements unless certified by a trusted authority

“I didn’t know” is not a valid excuse.
Strict liability applies as you are responsible for everything you put in your body.

Sanctions for Violations

Violating anti-doping rules can result in:
  • Suspension from all MMA-SFI sanctioned events and training camps
  • Disqualification of results and titles
  • Removal from Team India squads or international representation
  • Permanent ban for repeat or serious offences

Support for Clean Athletes

MMA-SFI supports athletes who choose the hard, honest road. We provide:
  • Direct access to the current WADA Prohibited List
  • Anti-doping education materials in English and regional languages
  • A channel to report doping-related misconduct safely and anonymously
  • TUE support for athletes with legitimate medical needs

A Message to Athletes

You may win with shortcuts. But you don’t deserve to.
Every punch you throw, every round you survive – let it be earned.
Because real fighters don’t cheat. They train harder.
At MMA-SFI. We fight clean.

MMA-SFI (Mixed Martial Arts Sports Federation of India) is the national governing body for MMA in India.
