[H6] About Us
[H2] Who We Are
[P] MMA-SFI (Mixed Martial Arts Sports Federation of India) is the national governing body for MMA in India. We are committed to building a transparent, athlete-first structure that supports Indian fighters from the grassroots to the global stage. Affiliated with AMMA (Asian Mixed Martial Arts Association) and GAMMA (Global Authority for Mixed Martial Arts) , MMA-SFI operates with international standards to ensure fair competition, certified coaching and officiating and fighter welfare at every level. We aim to organise sanctioned district, state, and national-level championships, provide training and certification for officials and work to create legitimate pathways for Indian athletes to represent the country in continental and world championships.
[P] At our core, we believe that Indian fighters deserve more than just a platform-they deserve a system that supports their dreams with structure, safety, and opportunity.
IMG: https://mma-sfi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/logo-black.webp
[H3] Vision
[P] Our vision is to build a strong, structured future for Indian MMA - where fighters from every background get the opportunity, resources and recognition they deserve. We aim to uplift Indian athletes by supporting them with quality coaching, scientific training methods, proper nutrition, medical safety and access to national and international platforms. Our goal is clear: to develop world-class fighters who bring medals home and make India a respected name in global MMA. We are working to ensure that every fighter, whether from a big city or a small town has a fair shot at success-through transparent systems, certified officials and a professional fight infrastructure. MMA-SFI stands for progress, transparency and athlete-first governance so that Indian MMA is no longer just growing, but thriving.
[H3] Mission
[P] MMA-SFI is committed to developing a strong, transparent, and athlete-focused structure for Mixed Martial Arts in India. We aim to uplift Indian fighters by setting national standards, certifying coaches and officials, organising fair competitions, and ensuring access to quality training, nutrition, and medical care. Our mission is to create a clear pathway for Indian athletes to grow from grassroots to global platforms — with integrity, safety, and excellence at every step.
[H2] Core Objectives of MMA - SFI
[H3] Talent Development
[P] Identify and nurture MMA athletes from grassroots to elite level across all regions of India.
[H3] Standardised Coaching &amp; Officiating
[P] Build a national framework for certifying coaches, referees and judges to ensure fairness, safety and consistency in the sport.
[H3] Structured Competitions
[P] Organise and sanction MMA events at district, state, and national levels with transparent rankings and selection systems.
[H3] Fighter Welfare
[P] Ensure fighters have access to proper training facilities, nutrition guidance, injury management, insurance and mental health support.
[H3] International Exposure
[P] Facilitate opportunities for Indian athletes to compete and succeed on international platforms under globally recognised bodies.
[H3] Inclusion &amp; Accessibility
[P] Promote equal opportunities for athletes regardless of gender, background, or region thus ensuring MMA reaches every corner of the country.

[H2] Anti-Doping Policy – MMA SFI
[A] Home
[H3] We Fight Clean. Always.
[P] MMA is built on discipline, honour and fair play. At MMA-SFI, we believe that no victory is worth compromising your integrity. Our Anti-Doping Policy ensures that every athlete competing under our banner plays by the same rules without shortcuts, without banned substances and without excuses.
[P] We are committed to protecting clean athletes, creating a level playing field and promoting long-term athlete health and safety.
IMG: https://mma-sfi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PIC7-1-1-scaled.jpg
[H3] What Our Anti-Doping Policy Covers
[LI] Prohibited Substances &amp; Methods : MMA-SFI follows the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) Prohibited List. All athletes must avoid performance-enhancing drugs, masking agents or any method that manipulates testing or results.
[LI] Testing at Events : Doping control may be conducted at any MMA-SFI sanctioned event including National Championships, State Championships and official selection trials. Testing may be random or targeted.
[LI] Out-of-Competition Testing : Athletes selected for international representation or elite training programs may betested at any time, without prior notice.
[LI] Therapeutic Use Exemptions (TUE) : If you are required to take a medication that contains a banned substance for medical reasons, you must apply for a TUE in advance with proper documentation.
[LI] Education &amp; Awareness : MMA-SFI conducts regular sessions to educate athletes, coaches and support staff about anti-doping rules, safe supplement practices and the long-term risks of banned substances.
[H2] Athlete Responsibilities
[P] Submit to testing if selected and cooperate fully with officials
[P] Disclose all medications in advance and apply for a TUE when needed
[P] Refuse pressure or shortcuts offered by coaches, teammates or outsiders
[P] Know what substances and methods are banned
[P] Avoid all supplements unless certified by a trusted authority
[P] “I didn’t know” is not a valid excuse. Strict liability applies as you are responsible for everything you put in your body.
[H3] Sanctions for Violations
[LI] Suspension from all MMA-SFI sanctioned events and training camps
[LI] Disqualification of results and titles
[LI] Removal from Team India squads or international representation
[LI] Permanent ban for repeat or serious offences
[H3] Support for Clean Athletes
[LI] Direct access to the current WADA Prohibited List
[LI] Anti-doping education materials in English and regional languages
[LI] A channel to report doping-related misconduct safely and anonymously
[LI] TUE support for athletes with legitimate medical needs
[H3] A Message to Athletes
[P] You may win with shortcuts. But you don’t deserve to. Every punch you throw, every round you survive &#8211; let it be earned. Because real fighters don’t cheat. They train harder. At MMA-SFI. We fight clean.

[H2] Safeguarding at MMA-SFI
[A] Home
[H3] Our Athletes' Safety Comes First - Always
[P] MMA-SFI is committed to creating a safe, respectful and abuse-free environment for every individual involved in the sport whether they’re athletes, coaches, officials, support staff or volunteers.
[P] As a newly formed body laying the foundation for Indian MMA’s future, we are actively building safeguarding systems and policies that will protect children, women, vulnerable adults and all participants in our ecosystem. This is a priority, not an afterthought.
IMG: https://mma-sfi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/PIC7-1-1-scaled.jpg
[H3] What Safeguarding Means to Us
[P] Safeguarding is about protecting people from harm including physical, emotional, sexual and psychological abuse-in any setting connected to our sport.
[P] It also includes:
[LI] Preventing bullying, harassment or exploitation
[LI] Ensuring athletes, coaches, officials and support staff behave responsibly
[LI] Creating environments where participants feel safe to speak up
[LI] Educating everyone involved about boundaries, respect and accountability
[H2] Our Current Focus
[P] Working towards drafting a Code of Conduct for all registered coaches, officials, athletes and support staff
[P] Developing reporting procedures that are accessible and anonymous
[P] Identifying safeguarding points of contact at all major events starting soon
[P] Working towards setting up a formal Safeguarding Committee (POSH) to handle complaints and concerns
[P] Starting awareness sessions (online and offline) for academies and state affiliates
[H3] What We Expect from Our Members
[P] All affiliated academies, coaches and state units will soon be required to:
[LI] Sign and uphold MMA-SFI’s Safeguarding &amp; Code of Conduct Policy
[LI] Appoint a designated safeguarding contact person within their academy or state
[LI] Report any suspected misconduct or abuse to MMA-SFI immediately
[LI] Cooperate with any investigations or reviews conducted by the federation
[P] Any action or behaviour that puts any individual connected to the sport especially minors and women at risk will be taken seriously and may result in suspension, de-affiliation or a permanent ban.
[H3] How to Report a Concern (Starting Soon)
[P] We’re currently setting up our Safeguarding Cell. In the meantime, concerns can be raised directly with the central MMA-SFI team via email:
[P] Email: admin@mmasfi.org (All reports will be handled confidentially)
[P] You may report:
[LI] Harassment or misconduct by a coach, official, athlete or staff member
[LI] Concerns about athlete safety or wellbeing
[LI] Suspected abuse or inappropriate behaviour
[LI] Unsafe training environments
[H3] Building a Safer Sport - Together
[P] We’re still a young federation but we believe the duty to protect comes before performance or results. As our structure grows, safeguarding will be at the centre of how we train, compete and lead.
[P] Every athlete deserves to grow in an environment that values safety, dignity and respect.
[P] MMA-SFI is committed to making that the standard.
