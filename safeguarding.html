Our Athletes' Safety Comes First – Always

MMA-SFI is committed to creating a safe, respectful, and abuse-free environment for every individual involved in the sport — whether they are athletes, coaches, referees, cutmen, or volunteers.

As the national governing body laying the foundation for Indian MMA’s future, we are actively implementing safeguarding systems and policies that will protect everyone in our combat sports community, with particular emphasis on minors and female athletes.

What Safeguarding Means to Us

Safeguarding is about proactively protecting people from harm — including physical, emotional, sexual, and psychological abuse — in any gym, camp, locker room, or tournament setting connected to MMA.

  • Preventing bullying, harassment, and exploitation: Establishing zero-tolerance protocols for any form of intimidation or abuse.
  • Accountable Staff: Ensuring all coaches, corners, cutmen, referees, and team managers adhere to strict professional boundaries.
  • Safe Speech Environments: Creating trusted channels where participants feel completely protected and encouraged to speak up without fear of retaliation.
  • Education on Respect: Mandatory sensitivity training regarding consent, athlete dignity, and mental well-being across state units.

Our Current Focus

Code of Conduct

Enforcing mandatory signed Code of Conduct agreements for all registered coaches, officials, judges, and athletes.

POSH Committee

Establishing a formal Safeguarding & POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) Committee led by women directors and legal advocates.

Event Safety Officers

Designating official on-ground safeguarding contacts and certified medical doctors at every sanctioned championship ring/cage.

What We Expect from Our Members

All affiliated state federations, registered academies, and clubs must uphold these core obligations:

  • Sign and uphold MMA-SFI’s Safeguarding & Code of Conduct Policy.
  • Appoint a designated safeguarding contact person within their academy or state branch.
  • Report any suspected misconduct or abuse to the MMA-SFI Ethics Committee immediately.
  • Cooperate transparently with any independent reviews or investigations conducted by the federation.

How to Report a Concern

Confidential Reporting Channel

Concerns or violations regarding athlete safety, harassment, or unsafe conditions can be reported directly to our central Safeguarding Cell in complete confidence:

admin@mmasfi.org (All reports handled confidentially by our Ethics Committee)

Building a Safer Sport – Together

"We believe the duty to protect comes before performance or results. Every athlete deserves to train and compete in an environment that values safety, dignity, and respect."
