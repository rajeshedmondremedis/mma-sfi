About Us

Who We Are

MMA-SFI (Mixed Martial Arts Sports Federation of India) is the national governing body for MMA in India. We are committed to building a transparent, athlete-first structure that supports Indian fighters from the grassroots to the global stage.

Vision

Our vision is to build a strong, structured future for Indian MMA - where fighters from every background get the opportunity, resources and recognition they deserve.

Mission

MMA-SFI is committed to developing a strong, transparent, and athlete-focused structure for Mixed Martial Arts in India. We aim to uplift Indian fighters by setting national standards,

Core Objectives of MMA-SFI

1. Talent Development
2. Standardised Coaching & Officiating
3. Structured Competitions

President's Message

Our commitment is to build a strong and unified MMA community in India. Through athlete development, transparent governance, quality coaching, and international opportunities, we aim to empower Indian fighters to achieve excellence on the national and global stage.
