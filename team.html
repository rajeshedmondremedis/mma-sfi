Meet the Team

Home Team
Federation Board

Discipline. Direction. Development

The executive board and technical committee bringing world-class expertise in sports governance, combat coaching, athletic safeguarding, and legal integrity to MMA in India.

MR. Nikhil Kunder

Board Member & Senior Coach

Nikhil Kunder brings over 19 years of experience in the fitness and combat sports industry and holds an MBA in Marketing. He has been actively involved in Mixed Martial Arts governance and athlete conditioning.

As a coach, he has consistently produced national champions, with many of his athletes competing at prestigious international championships representing Team India.

MR. Jitendra Khare

National Head Coach & Technical Director

Mr. Jitendra Khare is one of the most respected pioneer figures in Indian mixed martial arts, with a long-standing commitment to the sport’s nationwide technical development.

He has served as the Indian National MMA Team Coach at multiple World and Asian championships, leading athletes to historic podium finishes and medal triumphs for the nation.

MR. Pranav Chatre

Business Strategist & Board Member

Mr. Pranav Chatre is an experienced business strategist with over 20 years in the education, wellness, and green energy sectors, driving sustainable federation operations.

He holds a Bachelor of Engineering from Mumbai University and an MA in Yoga, and is currently pursuing a PhD in Yoga from JJT University, championing athletic balance and mental discipline.

Mr. Ashutosh Pratap Singh

Vice President | Head - North Zone & PR

Mr. Ashutosh Pratap Singh is a prominent young social and sports activist, currently serving as National Vice-President of Mixed Martial Arts Sports Federation of India.

Having served as a National Convener of Khelo Bharat, he works tirelessly toward grassroots youth athletic access, building structured platforms for rising combat talent across North India.

Ms. Preksha Jhaveri

Vice President | Head - Technical Committee

Ms. Preksha Jhaveri has extensive experience in fitness, sports, and media. In 2018 she made history as a founding partner at X1 International and has managed Team India at international tournaments.

A 1st Dan Black Belt, AMMA Level Examiner, Certified Level C Judge for GAMMA and AMMA, and National Judge, she directs technical standards and officiating compliance.

CA. Prachiti Nagpurkar

Vice President | Ethics, Safeguarding & Women

Prachiti Nagpurkar is a Chartered Accountant by qualification and a dedicated lifelong athlete. She is a certified B-Level Judge and has coached athletes at National and World championships.

As Vice President, she leads MMA-SFI's Safeguarding and Women’s Commission, advocating for female participation, athlete protection, self-defense, and ethical conduct.

ADV. Rutu Pawar

Vice President | Head - Legal

Mr. Rutu Pawar is a practicing advocate with substantial experience in civil, commercial, and sports arbitration litigation, regularly appearing before high courts and tribunals.

Known for his strategic clarity in legal argumentation, he ensures MMA-SFI maintains ironclad governance, statutory compliance, and athlete contract transparency.

Mr. Susovan Ghosh

Joint Secretary | Ethics & Disciplinary

Susovan Ghosh is recognized across Indian combat sports for his technical expertise, international competition pedigree, and dedication to martial values.

As part of the coaching contingent under the Olympic Council of Asia (OCA), Susovan serves as Joint Secretary and Head of Ethics, upholding clean combat principles.

MR. Rahul Chavan

Joint Secretary | Events & Media

Mr. Rahul Chavan is a seasoned marketing strategist with over a decade of experience in brand development, national event production, and broadcast media management.

He spearheads championship logistics, tournament production, and athlete storytelling to bring Indian MMA into mainstream sports spotlight.

MMA-SFI (Mixed Martial Arts Sports Federation of India) is the national governing body for MMA in India, championing athletes across all states and international federations.
