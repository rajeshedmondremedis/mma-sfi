National Network

Uniting All States Under One Banner

MMA-SFI is building India’s most structured and legitimate platform for Mixed Martial Arts. We are opening doors to dedicated regional leaders, martial arts academies, and state sports associations to establish recognized state chapters.

If you are already active in the MMA space and want to bring your state under the national framework, state affiliation with MMA-SFI gives your athletes direct, verified access to national selection trials, certified officiating seminars, and continental championships.

Apply as a State Federation

State bodies must be registered non-profit societies or trusts committed to democratic sports management, athlete safeguarding, and organizing annual official state championships.

  • Registered state society registration documents
  • Elected executive body and technical head
  • Compliance with MMA-SFI Code of Conduct and Anti-Doping
Submit State Dossier

Apply as Individual / Academy

Fighters, independent academies, certified cutmen, and coaches can affiliate directly to obtain official national licensing and participate in sanctioned ranking bouts.

  • Official Fighter / Coach ID Card
  • Medical clearance record maintenance
  • Access to national ranking tournaments
